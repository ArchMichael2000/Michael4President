Research Boundary

Policies establish direction.The Policy Book states the institutions, rights, duties, and governing mechanisms being proposed.
Studies test affordability.Fiscal work examines whether the combined architecture can fund its transitions and sustain reliable government.
Models continue to develop.New sources and alternate funding arrangements may change the accounting without changing the policies themselves.

The completed work supports the conclusion that the general policy architecture can produce a more robust revenue base and that sustained fiscal surpluses are possible and likely. The downloadable studies carry the calculations; the Policy Book carries the program.

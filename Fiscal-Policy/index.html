The fiscal program has to show who pays, what is funded, and which money is available for each obligation. A household's HSA, a retirement contribution, and Treasury revenue belong to different owners or purposes. Combining the national accounts must preserve those distinctions.

Generative Sovereignty supplies the philosophical foundation for the program. Its concern with what a people can sustain across generations reaches public finance through the treatment of debt, essential services, and public assets. The figures here explain the financial mechanisms. They follow Innovative Tax Plan Overhaul V27 and Deep Tax Burden Study V35.

One liquid income definition

The model's liquid personal-income base is $20.471 trillion. It includes covered cash income available to the individual, such as wages, previously untaxed retirement distributions, royalties, taxable settlements, and foreign cash income. Social Security benefits, public assistance, and the return of after-tax principal receive their specified exclusions.

The standard individual income-tax rate is ten percent. Citizens contribute three percent to Social Security. Their HSA contributions use the two, three, and five percent tiers, with federal supplements supplying the specified citizen-side funding floor.

The full-time-student wage exemption and the qualifying youth and elderly rules retain their stated treatment. V35's aggregate calculation applies ten percent to its active liquid-income base; a household's treatment depends on its statutory classification. The common base keeps the same covered income identifiable across the personal tax and dedicated contributions.

The Well Being Tax and dedicated contributions

The Well Being Tax names the personal and compensation-based contributions supporting Social Security and healthcare. The receipts remain assigned to their separate ledgers. NCC is a different assessment, based on company value.

Employers contribute three percent of compensation transferred to citizen employees for Social Security. Covered compensation includes cash wages, bonuses, stock, and other equity awards at their specified fair value. State and local governments contribute as employers on the same terms. Their compensation is recorded once in the national base.

Under the book's baseline citizenship system, non-citizens use a Resident Contributor Number and contribute at seven percent, with a separate seven percent employer contribution. The money supports the Social Security trust under the rules in Citizen-Only Social Security. The Two-Tier Citizenship proposal in Hyper-Reform supplies its own terms if enacted.

Abolishing the legacy payroll tax therefore accompanies specified replacement contributions. The employer obligation still follows compensation. NCC follows enterprise value. The accounts need to show the difference so hiring, compensation, and company valuation do not become interchangeable descriptions of the same tax base.

Corporate income and productive deployment

The corporate statutory rate is fifteen percent. V35 uses a ten percent effective Treasury rate after the active reinvestment, bonus, and productive-deployment provisions. A qualifying use must correspond to the business activity or employee benefit established in law. The reductions and minimum obligation belong alongside the rate in any account of what a company owes.

Estate and trust distributions receive separate three percent treatment. Covered entity-level deferral receives ten percent source-level treatment, with credits coordinating later distributions of the same economic income. The credit records what has already been paid when the income reaches a later tax event.

Explicit adjustments account for overlapping collection channels so the same receipt is counted once.

The Protectorate Fee calculation uses one percent of the covered expenditure base of seventy-seven countries. Fair Profit Tax follows the policybook's product-cost multiple mechanism. The workbook records the separate calculation bases.

Social Security and National Continuity

NCC applies a 0.73 percent assessment to the enterprise value of covered companies above the one-million-dollar threshold. V35's central Year 0 covered base is $68 trillion, producing $496.4 billion. The model uses a 1.2 percent annual growth assumption for the contribution and valuation bases.

NCC first fills the remaining Social Security gap. Its residual passes to the National Inheritance and Reserve Fund. At Year 10, the model projects $559.290 billion in NCC receipts: $181.224 billion for Social Security and $378.065 billion for NIRF.

Social Security also receives its dedicated contributions, investment income, and assigned lending earnings. Healthcare keeps its own account. Routine NHDC support for Social Security is zero in the active National Continuity structure.

V27 separately sets out treatment for qualified family farms and closely held manufacturing: a five-million-dollar allowance, a quarter-percent band through ten million dollars, and the full rate above it. V35 keeps its central headline calculation alongside a separate carve-out sensitivity schedule. The design section and the headline case should remain identifiable when their figures are used.

Healthcare and public capacity

The healthcare account includes citizen HSAs, federal HSA supplements, employer and public sponsor funding, assigned sin-tax receipts, and external NHDC earnings. The individual ownership and public purpose of these funds continue to apply within the aggregate account.

V35 begins with $5.300 trillion in annual national health expenditure. Its twenty percent structural-savings case produces a $4.240 trillion cost basis. Domestic provider margins remain within the system, external funding is counted separately, and international licensing enters the consolidated healthcare balance.

The NHDC waterfall maintains a $120 billion healthcare reserve and provides for approved research and productive expansion. The remaining distributable amount is divided equally between NIRF and the Citizen Wealth Fund. The account must show which obligations have been met before that distribution is made.

Land ownership and the land account

The land-value tax falls on land, with the primary-family-home and qualifying small-business protections maintained. Improvements remain distinct from the land beneath them. The model carries a $2.233 trillion annual land-revenue target.

The target is presented against an $86.145 trillion real-estate envelope. It equals 2.592 percent of that envelope. The central sensitivity treats forty percent as land and fifty percent of that land as taxable, giving a $17.229 trillion base and a 12.960 percent implied annual rate.

The account must preserve the difference between land and structures, apply exemptions, and identify which government receives the money. Property-tax replacement also requires an account of the services each level of government continues to finance. A revenue target alone does not allocate those responsibilities.

Household effects

V35's illustrations compare 2024 single-filer federal income and employee payroll taxes with the proposed personal tax, citizen Social Security contribution, and assigned HSA tier.

Fiscal stewardship over time

The operating projection describes recurring replacement operations. Startup capitalization, construction, and the delivery schedule belong in a transition appropriation. Keeping those accounts identifiable shows what is needed to establish the system and what it expects to sustain afterward.

Borrowing to acquire an asset creates a liability as well as an asset. An investment return becomes available for use under the fund's withdrawal rules. A protected balance keeps its purpose when national accounts are consolidated.

Appropriation must also meet the practical requirements of delivery. Skilled labor, materials, energy, and maintenance have to be available when a project needs them. The financial plan should be examined with that delivery account, so an authorized expenditure can become a service the country is able to keep providing.

Questions and answers

Which part of the surplus can fund ordinary government?

General Government's operating balance is the separate $318.956 billion line. The consolidated total also contains Healthcare's balance, the NCC residual assigned to NIRF, and a protected-capital receipt. Their destinations continue to apply when the accounts are combined.

Why distinguish the Well Being Tax from NCC?

The Well Being Tax follows liquid personal income and compensation, with receipts assigned separately to Social Security and healthcare. NCC follows covered company value. It closes the remaining Social Security gap and sends its residual to NIRF. Those different bases and destinations must remain identifiable in the account.

Whose money is in an HSA?

The household owns its HSA funds. Citizen contributions, public supplements, and assigned healthcare receipts have different places within the overall healthcare account. An aggregate funding total must preserve ownership and purpose, so household funds do not become an apparent addition to unrestricted Treasury revenue.

Where do construction and startup costs appear?

They belong in the transition appropriation. The operating projection describes recurring operations at the selected replacement stage. Startup capitalization and the schedule by which capacity enters service need their own account. Borrowing to acquire an asset also creates a liability that remains visible alongside it.
